## The mobile app prototype

Built 2026-11-18: `app/etihad-everywhere-app.html`, a third deliverable (besides the presentation and the console) showing the customer side inside the bank app. It mirrors the app's Hub tab from the user's screenshots (Products, Investment, Lifestyle, Insurance; Payment/Home/Hub bar; light theme in the app's own look) and adds a unique "Etihad Everywhere" tile in Lifestyle (burgundy-to-orange, pin with a radar pulse and a glint). Tapping it opens a full screen with four tabs: For you (October cashback, missed savings, next-rank gain, rank table, vault "move to my account", plan advice with a comparison sheet and switch/undo), Near me (offers by distance with a pay-here calculator: Fraij 100 JOD gives 40.60 JOD, and the 0% Easy Payment calculator), Alerts (smart notifications with "Why did I get this?" and "Not interested" with undo) and Privacy (consent switches kept on the device; turning analysis off empties For you). The persona Sara's numbers use the same model and REF values as the page (Rank 3, Standard, best plan Essential +2.01 JOD). No rank progress bar is shown because Rank 2/1 thresholds are unknown. Consent and location (2026-11-18): opening the tile the first time shows a two-step popup (choose location / card spend / offers, then confirm; saved on the device as `ee_confirmed`); after confirming with location allowed, the app requests the real device location and opens Near me with a map drawn on the device (no map tiles, so the position never reaches a third party): blue dot with accuracy ring and offer pins placed by distance and bearing; tapping a pin opens that offer's calculator. Where location is refused (the claude.ai frame blocks it for this account), it says so and offers a clearly labelled sample location in Shmeisani, Amman. Turning location off in Privacy forgets the position at once. It is marked as a prototype with synthetic data and has no login or card fields. Connected to the console (2026-11-18, user: "all app changes reflected on the console, and the console controls and shows the results"): the console publishes the app beside itself as `app.html` (publish with `files: {"app.html": "app/etihad-everywhere-app.html"}` every time the app changes), fetches it, adds `<!doctype html>` and runs it in an iframe (`srcdoc`) on the Customer App screen. A `postMessage` bridge links them: console → app `ee-state` (the selected customer's data and current plan, consent and confirmed flag, approved offers only, bank alerts from `ops/appAlerts` plus sent outbox messages, the stop switch, REF assumptions); app → console `ee-event` (consent with source, location result without coordinates, offer-view / offer-calc, plan-switch with undo, vault-claim, keep-plan, alert-dismiss / undismiss / why). The console writes them to `ops/consents`, `ops/accounts` (plan override that feeds `runPipeline`, vault claimed, no plan offer for 3 months), `ops/appAlerts`, `ops/appEvents` (last 500) and the audit log, and shows them in the side panel's activity list, the overview KPI and the merchant report ("opened in the app"). Editors can send a location alert (checked by the offer agent: marketing and location consent, quiet hours, 2 a day, stop switch) and reset the consent screen. The Customer App screen refreshes only its side panel so the app never reloads; changing the customer pushes new state without a reload. View-only visitors see the app react but nothing is saved (one notice). Checks C23 (approved offers only + stop switch reach the app) and C24 (plan switch reaches decisions); 24/24. On its own (standalone link) the app keeps its local demo data. Location Agent (2026-11-18, user: "an AI agent to send the notification based on location, not manual, and based on his behavioural analysis"): manual alert sending was removed. The app geofences on the device (150 m around each approved shop; real GPS via `watchPosition`, or the "Walk around" demo in Near me) and emits `geofence` with shop id and distance only, never coordinates; a push banner shows new alerts. The console's Location Agent (`#agent`, `onGeofence`/`decideGeofence`) decides alone: autopilot on (`sys/state.autopilot`, default on), stop switch off, approved offer, location + marketing consent, quiet hours 22–08, 2 a day, 24 h per shop, shop not dismissed this week, no financing, and behavioural analysis `behaviourScore` ≥ 40/100 (spend share at similar shops, missed savings, card activity, shops opened in the app, dismissed alerts; every point has a logged reason). Claude writes with placeholders {shop}/{offer}/{distance} through `gateMessage` (template fallback, 12 s timeout), rules are checked again before delivery, decisions go to `ops/agentLog` and the audit log. Standalone app applies the same basic rules on the device. Check C25; 25/25. It imitates a real bank's app, so it was built as a file first; the user then asked to publish it (2026-11-18): https://claude.ai/artifact/Y3hDRqeYC7CFJh3acJKKJo (private at first publish; republish to that same URL). Always keep the prototype label.
